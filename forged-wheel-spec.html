MoreYi supplies forged 6061-T6 aluminum wheels from 15″ to 30″ in 83 catalogued sizes, single and dual wheel configurations, with load ratings from 1,000 kg up to 5,800 kg per wheel. Every part number below lists weight, load rating, inner/outer offset, mounting disc thickness, PCD, center bore, bolt-hole pattern, hand hole layout, tire size, maximum cold inflation pressure and valve type. All wheels are DOT / ECE / CCC certified. Part numbers containing H locate via center bore; S via bolt holes.
