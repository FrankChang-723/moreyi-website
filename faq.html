EN: OBM means the manufacturer sells under its own registered brand. OEM means it produces to your design and your brand. ODM means it designs and produces the product, which you then sell under your brand. MoreYi is an OBM — a manufacturer, not a trader — holding its own mold IP and working with 40+ certified factories, while also supporting OEM/ODM projects. 15. 什么是AQL 2.5验货标准？ / What is AQL 2.5 inspection standard?
AQL 2.5是ISO 2859-1抽样标准中的可接受质量限（Acceptable Quality Limit），即整批抽检时被判定合格所允许的最大缺陷水平。MoreYi执行三道质量关卡：SGS首件封样、出厂前100%全检、AQL 2.5第三方验货；若第三方验货不通过，退还定金。质量在货物离华前即完成验证，而非到港后才发现问题。
EN: AQL 2.5 is the Acceptable Quality Limit under the ISO 2859-1 sampling standard — the maximum defect level considered acceptable when a batch passes random-sampling inspection. MoreYi applies three quality gates: an SGS-sealed first-article sample, 100% factory inspection before release, and third-party inspection at AQL 2.5. If third-party inspection fails, your deposit is refunded. Quality is therefore verified before goods leave China, not after they arrive.
